I Spent 6 Hours Building an Angular OTP Component So You Don't Have To

What started as a simple OTP input turned into 6 hours of edge cases — auto-focus, backspace navigation, clipboard paste support, and clean Angular architecture. Sometimes the hardest problems are the small UX details users never notice when they work correctly.

This post walks through the thinking behind every design decision in the Night Sky OTP component — why auto-advancing inputs are trickier than they seem, how to handle paste events gracefully, and the state management approach that keeps the component clean as the complexity grows. It's a deep dive into the kind of frontend detail work that separates polished software from "good enough."
