Problems I Solve

If any of these sound familiar, I can help

"We're running everything on spreadsheets"

Complex spreadsheets break, don't scale, and make collaboration painful. I build web apps that replace them with something faster, more reliable and easier to use.

"Our systems don't talk to each other"

When your tools and platforms live in silos, data gets lost and work gets duplicated. I build APIs and integrations that connect everything together.

"I have an idea but no developer"

You know what you need but don't have the technical skills to build it. I take ideas from concept to working software — no jargon, no guesswork.

"We can't see what our data is telling us"

Raw data in databases and files isn't useful until you can see it clearly. I build dashboards that surface the numbers that matter to your business.

"Manual processes are slowing us down"

Repetitive tasks eat up time and introduce errors. I build tools that automate workflows so your team can focus on higher-value work.

"Our software feels outdated"

Legacy tools that are slow, clunky, or hard to use hold your team back. I modernise applications with current technology and better user experience.

How It Works

A straightforward process from your idea to deployed software

01

Share Your Idea

Tell me about the problem you're trying to solve or the tool you need. No technical knowledge required — just describe what you're dealing with.

02

Plan the Solution

I'll map out the approach — what to build, which technologies fit, and a realistic timeline. You'll know exactly what to expect before any code is written.

03

Build

Development happens in focused sprints with regular check-ins so you can see progress, give feedback and steer the direction as we go.

04

Test & Refine

Thorough testing across devices and scenarios. I fix issues, polish the experience and make sure everything works reliably before launch.

05

Deploy & Support

Your software goes live on reliable cloud infrastructure. I handle deployment and remain available for updates, fixes and new features as your needs evolve.
