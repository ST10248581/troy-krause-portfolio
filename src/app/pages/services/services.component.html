What I Build

Full-stack software solutions tailored to your business

I specialise in building custom software for businesses that have outgrown generic tools. Whether you need a web application to manage your operations, a dashboard to visualise your data, or an API to connect the systems you already use — I design, build and deploy it end to end.

Every project is different, but they all share the same approach: understand the problem first, plan a realistic solution, then build something that works reliably and is easy to use. No unnecessary complexity, no features you didn't ask for.

APIs & Integrations

RESTful APIs that connect your systems, automate data flow and eliminate the manual copy-paste work between your tools. I design clean, well-documented endpoints that make it easy for other systems to talk to your application reliably and securely.

Cloud-Native Solutions

MVPs & Prototypes

Turn your idea into a working product quickly — validate your concept with real users before committing to a full build. I help you identify the core features that matter most, build a functional version fast, and iterate based on real feedback rather than guesswork.

Custom Business Tools

Replace spreadsheets and manual processes with purpose-built software that fits the way your team actually works. From inventory trackers to client management systems, I build the internal tools that make your day-to-day operations smoother and more efficient.

Problems I Solve

If any of these sound familiar, I can help

Most of my projects start with a conversation about a pain point — not a technical specification. You don't need to know what tech stack you want or how databases work. You just need to tell me what's not working, and I'll figure out how to fix it.

Here are some of the common situations where custom software makes a real difference. If you see your own frustration in any of these, that's a good sign we should talk.

"We're running everything on spreadsheets"

Complex spreadsheets break, don't scale, and make collaboration painful. I build web apps that replace them with something faster, more reliable and easier to use. Your data stays structured, accessible and safe — without the version control nightmares of shared spreadsheets.

"Our systems don't talk to each other"

When your tools and platforms live in silos, data gets lost and work gets duplicated. I build APIs and integrations that connect everything together so information flows automatically between the systems you already rely on, without anyone having to manually move it.

"I have an idea but no developer"

You know what you need but don't have the technical skills to build it. I take ideas from concept to working software — no jargon, no guesswork. I'll help you define what needs to be built, break it into manageable phases, and deliver something you can actually use and show to others.

"We can't see what our data is telling us"

Raw data in databases and files isn't useful until you can see it clearly. I build dashboards that surface the numbers that matter to your business — with filtering, sorting and visualisation that lets your team make decisions based on real information instead of gut feeling.

"Manual processes are slowing us down"

Repetitive tasks eat up time and introduce errors. I build tools that automate workflows so your team can focus on higher-value work. Whether it's generating reports, processing incoming data or routing tasks to the right people — automation saves hours every week.

"Our software feels outdated"

Legacy tools that are slow, clunky, or hard to use hold your team back. I modernise applications with current technology and better user experience. Sometimes that means a full rebuild, sometimes it means targeted improvements — I'll help you work out which approach makes the most sense.
