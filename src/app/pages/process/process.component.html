How It Works

A straightforward process from your idea to deployed software

Working with an independent developer should feel simple and low-risk. I keep the process transparent so you always know what's happening, what's coming next, and how your investment is being spent. No disappearing for weeks — you'll see regular progress and have the chance to steer the direction at every stage.

Every project is different in scope and complexity, but the overall shape is the same. Here's how a typical engagement works from start to finish.

01

Share Your Idea

Tell me about the problem you're trying to solve or the tool you need. No technical knowledge required — just describe what you're dealing with. We'll have an initial conversation to understand the scope, the users and the goals behind the project.

02

Plan the Solution

I'll map out the approach — what to build, which technologies fit, and a realistic timeline. You'll know exactly what to expect before any code is written. This includes a clear breakdown of features, milestones and what the end result will look like.

03

Build

Development happens in focused sprints with regular check-ins so you can see progress, give feedback and steer the direction as we go. You won't be left wondering what's happening — I share updates, demos and working builds throughout the process.

04

Test & Refine

Thorough testing across devices and scenarios. I fix issues, polish the experience and make sure everything works reliably before launch. This includes both automated tests and hands-on review to catch the things automated tests miss.

05

Deploy & Support

Your software goes live on reliable cloud infrastructure. I handle deployment and remain available for updates, fixes and new features as your needs evolve. I set up monitoring and CI/CD so future changes can be shipped quickly and safely.

The timeline depends on the size of the project. A focused MVP might take a few weeks, while a more complex application could span a couple of months. I'll always give you a realistic estimate upfront — I'd rather be honest about timing than overpromise and underdeliver.

Technology

The tools and platforms I use to build reliable software

I choose technologies based on what works best for each project, but my core stack is the same set of tools I use professionally every day — .NET and C# on the backend, Angular and TypeScript on the frontend, SQL Server for data, and Azure for hosting. These are battle-tested, well-supported technologies that scale from small tools to enterprise applications.

I also have hands-on experience with a range of additional languages, frameworks and databases. The right tool depends on the job — and I'll always recommend the technology that makes the most sense for your specific project, not just the one I'm most comfortable with.
